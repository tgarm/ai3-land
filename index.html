Open-source tools for the Autonomys Network

Autonomys is a permanent, decentralized storage network; AI3 is its native token. The tools below are free-software, pure-frontend clients for it: no accounts, no backends — chain access goes straight from your browser to permissionless public infrastructure, and your keys stay in your wallet.

PermaDrive

Permanent file storage. Files are packed into IPLD DAGs in the browser and published directly to the Autonomys consensus chain — pay the one-time on-chain storage fee and the farmer network archives them permanently. No accounts, no credit expiry, manifest-based retrieval.

permadrive.ai3.land · permanent storage · pay per use

Open PermaDrive GitHub

XDM Bridge

Cross-domain AI3 transfers. Move tokens between the Autonomys consensus chain and the Auto EVM domain in both directions, straight from your wallet — with progress tracking for the cross-domain arrival window and paced RPC calls that respect public-node rate limits.

xdm-bridge.ai3.land · XDM protocol · pure frontend
