Open-source tools for the Autonomys Network

Autonomys is a decentralized layer-0 network: a consensus chain for permanent storage, attached domains for smart contracts, and AI3 as the native token that pays for it all. The basics, in three ideas:

Permanent storage

Files, text and app records published to the consensus chain are split into pieces and archived permanently by the farmer network: one-time fee, no subscriptions, no expiry. Content is addressed by its CID and stays retrievable from the Distributed Storage Network (DSN).

Proof-of-Archival-Storage

Farmers secure the chain not by burning energy but by storing its full archival history on everyday hardware — network security and permanent storage come from the same disk space.

Domains & XDM

Smart contracts run on Auto EVM, an EVM-compatible execution domain secured by the consensus chain; XDM is the cross-domain messaging that connects the two. The family's XDM Bridge is a pure-frontend client for exactly that.

Getting started

Get a wallet

SubWallet and Talisman are non-custodial wallets covering the Autonomys consensus chain and its Auto EVM domain. Install one, back up your seed phrase, and the ai3.land tools are ready to use.

Get AI3

AI3 pays for storage and gas, and trades on centralized exchanges. At the time of writing (2026-11), the AI3/USDT pair on XT.com is the active spot market — price trackers list the current venues.

Or earn it: farming

Farmers pledge disk space to secure the network and earn AI3 rewards; everyday hardware is enough. The official Space Acres app turns spare disk space into a farm in a few clicks — the docs cover setup end to end.
